# Ilustraciones editoriales KAIRUM

Serie original generada el 16 de octubre de 2026 con la herramienta nativa de generación de imágenes, en modo texto a imagen. No se copiaron imágenes ni logos de Anthropic, OpenAI o competidores. Las referencias editoriales son [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news) y [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/): jerarquía, espacio y protagonismo de la imagen. OpenAI pudo consultarse como índice de textos; su vista visual estuvo inicialmente limitada por verificación.

## Dirección

Papel marfil, carbón y amarillo KAIRUM; bordes recortados, textura material y sombras suaves. Objetos sencillos que expresan el tema de cada artículo. Sin robots, cerebros, interfaces falsas, cifras inventadas, texto rasterizado ni marcas ajenas. La figura explicativa dentro de cada lectura se construye en HTML: conserva texto seleccionable, traducciones y un pie con el alcance del ejemplo.

## Prompts utilizados

La guía se generó con este prompt completo:

> Create an original editorial cover illustration for KAIRUM, a thoughtful Spanish-language publication about how brands appear in AI answers. Landscape 3:2 composition, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no UI screenshots. Art direction: sophisticated tactile paper collage and dry charcoal drawing, warm ivory textured paper (#f5f2e9), bold luminous yellow (#ffe23b), deep charcoal black. Slightly imperfect hand-cut paper edges, subtle natural shadows, restrained ink grain, museum-quality contemporary editorial illustration; warm intelligent human craft, no shiny 3D plastic, no gradients, no generic AI brain or robot. Subject: measuring visibility means following evidence. Three ivory sheets arranged on a charcoal work surface; a single confident yellow ribbon threads through cut-out circular windows in the sheets, reveals a black ink star-like brand mark through the nearest aperture, and leads toward a small standing yellow disc at the far end. The paper layers and ribbon form a precise but poetic path of inquiry. Strong asymmetric composition, editorial negative space, excellent readable silhouette at small thumbnail sizes. Frame as a cohesive still life, cropped close, not a book mockup. No charts or fabricated results. Output only the full-bleed illustration.

### Señales

> Subject: three different signals must not be mistaken for one. On an ivory paper ground, three distinct sculptural paper objects follow a gentle left-to-right diagonal: a charcoal speech-bubble silhouette with a tiny yellow disk inside; a yellow interlocking paper chain with two substantial loops; a charcoal open doorway with a yellow strip entering its threshold. Each object stands alone, with small gaps between them and subtle yellow paper scraps tying the palette together. These are physical poetic symbols for a mention, a citation and a visit; not a literal funnel or guaranteed progression. Elegant highly crafted composition with ample negative space.

### Preguntas

> Subject: choosing questions is choosing a direction. Overhead still life on ivory textured paper: a charcoal branching path drawn with bold imperfect ink lines connects a loose arrangement of seven thick blank paper cards. Only one carefully selected route is traced by a vivid yellow paper ribbon. One larger central card has a round aperture revealing a charcoal disk underneath. A small yellow paper pointer at an intersection suggests a deliberate choice. No text or arrows printed on cards. Rich tactile layers, calm editorial composition, clear focal point, not a software flowchart.

### Resultados

> Subject: context changes how the same observation looks. A sophisticated overhead paper still life: two black circular paper frames of clearly different outer diameter lie side by side on warm ivory paper. Both have an identically sized yellow circular paper disk placed at their centers, with a large empty charcoal area around the disk in the larger frame and a smaller charcoal surround in the smaller frame. A thin hand-cut ivory strip links the two centers and a loose yellow curved strip rests nearby. The identical yellow disks are the key visual relationship: same evidence, different frame. Physical paper shadows, bold simple forms, charcoal grain, editorial restraint, no chart labels, no numbers, no misleading growth arrow.
